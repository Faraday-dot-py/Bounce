A 13k-parameter learned emulator (one token per ball: radius-graph attention, GRU, wall head, pair-impulse head) stepping a 100x100 arena. Inference is plain JavaScript. Cubes are the network's activations for the selected ball.

Click empty space (hold to stream) to add balls, click a ball to inspect it. Keys: space pause, . step, c clear, r reset, e attention edges, g ground truth, w weights. Faithfulness: this is a learned emulator (soup B, ~13k parameters), not the physics. Energy drifts and trajectories diverge from the true physics after roughly 20 steps; very dense piles are outside its training distribution and are caught by a containment guard. Colour is a blue/orange diverging map, symmetric about zero (about 0.5 for the gates). Source and weights: Faraday-dot-py/Bounce.
